## 🧠 About Me

> I build and examine AI agent systems through a service workflow project, with a focus on evidence, permissions, durable results, and evaluation.

- 🔭 Building a [digital front desk Agent](https://github.com/wanghanyu654321-cell/-agent) for customer questions, ticket creation, and human handoff
- 🧭 Keeping answers tied to approved, scoped knowledge rather than treating every retrieved document as usable evidence
- 🛡️ Putting identity, tenant and store scope, and tool permissions in server-side logic
- 🧪 Testing safety, retrieval, and business outcomes with an evaluation harness and CI gates
- 📚 Recording architecture, debugging, and code-ownership learning in [Agent / FDE notes](https://github.com/wanghanyu654321-cell/xuexi)
